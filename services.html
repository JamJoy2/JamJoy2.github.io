What we do

Services

Every job quoted before I touch it. Parts at cost, no markup. If a fix costs more than the machine is worth, I'll tell you. By appointment in Docklands — no walk-ins.

Custom PC Builds
Tell me the budget and what it's for — gaming, editing, a silent media box. I spec it, source the parts, and build it, then run FurMark, Cinebench and CrystalDiskInfo before it leaves. Cabled front and back. I'll demo it running when you collect it.
From $150 labour
PC Repair & Diagnostics
No POST, random BSODs, crashing under load, coil whine, thermal throttling — book it in and I'll find it. Tested with FurMark, MemTest86, CrystalDiskInfo, and a known-good PSU to swap against. Quote before any work starts. Sometimes the fix is a BIOS setting, and I'll just tell you that.
From $60 diagnostic
Hardware Upgrades
RAM, SSD, GPU, CPU swaps. I'll tell you first if it's worth it — a fast GPU next to an old quad-core just moves the bottleneck, and a SATA SSD in a board that takes NVMe leaves speed on the table. Fitted, tested, and benchmarked against the old part.
From $40 install
OS Install & Setup
Clean Windows 11 install — not a factory image with antivirus trials and Candy Crush pre-loaded. Drivers from the manufacturer, not Windows Update's best guess. Activated with your key. Data brought across from your old drive if you need it.
$60 flat
Cable Management
The bird's nest behind the motherboard tray is choking your airflow. I redo it front and back, Velcro instead of zip ties so the next upgrade isn't a fight. Sleeved extensions and 3D-printed combs if you want it to look the part.
From $50
PC Health Check
I run the machine hard for an afternoon — FurMark and Cinebench for temps, CrystalDiskInfo for drive health and remaining life, MemTest86 if the RAM looks shaky. You get the actual numbers back, not a green tick. Repaste and a dust-out included if it needs one.
$50 flat
Secure Data Wiping
ATA Secure Erase — a firmware-level wipe that clears the drive at the physical level, not just the file table. NIST 800-88 compliant, with a Certificate of Data Erasure naming the method used per drive. For SSDs this is the method the manufacturer intends; endless overwrite passes just wear the cells for nothing.
$50/drive · Bulk from $40
Thermal Paste Replacement
Old paste goes hard and temps climb. Fresh paste applied to suit the chip — a blob on a big Ryzen heatspreader, a thin spread on a bare die. On something that's been cooking you'll usually get 8–12°C back. If you don't, it's the cooler mount or the airflow, and I'll say so rather than charge you twice.
$30 CPU / $50 GPU
