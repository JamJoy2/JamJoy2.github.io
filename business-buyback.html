The Service

How the buyback works

This service is aimed at businesses replacing equipment, closing an office, or clearing out a storeroom of old IT gear. Rather than listing individual items or sourcing multiple buyers, you deal with one point of contact for the whole lot — from quote through to pickup and a data destruction certificate.

Paid on collection
