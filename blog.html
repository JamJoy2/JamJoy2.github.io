Build Log

Builds, repairs, and behind the scenes.

A running log of everything that comes through the workshop in Docklands.

James

PC builder · Docklands, Melbourne

I've been building PCs for over 7 years — started by fixing free pc's from my local e-waste at age 15, slowly got better, eventually started building for other people. Now I am running a small operation out of Docklands doing custom builds, repairs, and upgrades for everyone from gamers to small businesses.

Every build gets stress tested before it leaves — FurMark, Cinebench, CrystalDiskInfo. No exceptions. This log is where I document builds, interesting repairs, and the occasional breakdown of why I chose certain parts.
