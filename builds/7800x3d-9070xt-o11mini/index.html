About this build

Why it's built like this

Freshly built from used and new parts, with every component cleaned, stress-tested and validated. The Ryzen 7 7800X3D and Sapphire Pulse RX 9070 XT 16GB are a strong combo for 4K and high-refresh 1440p. The O11 Dynamic Mini doesn't support a 360mm radiator by default, so I designed and 3D printed custom spacer brackets to fit the Corsair Titan 360 RX LCD. That gives full 360mm cooling and the LCD pump in a much smaller case. No cheap supporting parts either: an X870 board, a 1000W SFX PSU and a Samsung 980 Pro.
